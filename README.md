📊 Customer Analysis Dashboard 

📁 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Customer Analysis Dashboard built using Power BI.
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer behavior, revenue patterns, and sales performance across multiple dimensions such as region, age group, state, category, and gender.
The goal of this project is to help businesses make data-driven decisions by understanding who their customers are, where revenue comes from, and how sales vary over time.

🎯 Key Insights
📍 Revenue Percentage by Region
•	South contributes the highest share of revenue.
•	Midwest, West, and Northeast follow with varying proportions.
👥 Age-wise Sales
•	Highest sales recorded in the 30–40 and 60–70 age groups.
•	Lower sales in <20 and >70 segments.
🗺 Revenue Per State (USA Map)
•	Strong revenue generation from major states such as Texas, California, and New York.
•	Several states show moderate to low contribution.
📈 Month-wise Revenue
•	Revenue fluctuates significantly across months.
•	Notable peaks observed in Dec 2020 (57K) and Apr 2021 (36K).
📦 Revenue by Category & Gender
•	Top category: Mobiles & Tablets
•	Comparison of male vs female spending across various product categories.
